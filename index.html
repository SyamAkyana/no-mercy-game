How to Play

Basics

  • Play a card matching the color or number/symbol of the top discard.
  • Wild cards can be played anytime; choose a color.
  • If you can't play, draw from the pile until you get a playable card.
  • First player to empty their hand wins!

Action Cards

  • Draw 2 / Draw 4 (colored): Next player draws and skips.
  • Reverse: Changes direction. With 2 players, acts as Skip.
  • Skip: Next player loses their turn.
  • Skip Everyone: All others skipped; you go again!
  • Discard All: Discard ALL your cards matching this card's color.

Wild Cards

  • Wild: Choose any color.
  • Wild Draw 6: Choose color; next player draws 6.
  • Wild Draw 10: Choose color; next player draws 10.
  • Wild Reverse Draw 4: Reverses direction; next player draws 4.
  • Wild Color Roulette: Next player chooses a color, draws until they get it.

Stacking

  • Facing a draw penalty? Stack by playing a draw card of equal or higher value.
  • Can't stack? Draw the entire accumulated penalty.

The 7's Swap

  • Play a 7 and swap your entire hand with another player.

The 0's Pass

  • Play a 0 and ALL players pass hands to the next player in direction.

UNO!

  • With 1 card left, tap UNO before your turn ends!
  • Miss it and draw 2 penalty cards.

Mercy Rule

  • Reach 25+ cards and you're eliminated! Last standing wins.
